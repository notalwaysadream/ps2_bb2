# Public Health Guideline Citation: Maternal & Newborn Danger Signs

## Official Source
- **Title**: *Module 6 & 7: Skills That Save Lives - Home Based Newborn Care & Maternal Health*
- **Publisher**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India / National Health Mission (NHM).
- **Reference URL**: [MoHFW ASHA Training Modules](https://nhm.gov.in)

---

## 1. Pregnancy & Postpartum Danger Signs (Immediate Referral to PHC/FRU)
1. **Severe Vaginal Bleeding**: Any antepartum or postpartum hemorrhage.
2. **Severe Headache & Blurred Vision**: Symptoms of pre-eclampsia / eclampsia.
3. **High Fever with Foul-Smelling Discharge**: Puerperal sepsis / systemic infection.
4. **Convulsions / Fits**: Eclampsia emergency.
5. **Severe Abdominal Pain**: Threat of uterine rupture or ectopic pregnancy.

---

## 2. Newborn Danger Signs (Immediate Referral to SNCU/PHC)
1. **Inability to Feed**: Baby stops sucking or cannot breastfeed.
2. **High Fever or Hypothermia**: Body hot to touch (>37.5°C) or abnormally cold (<35.5°C).
3. **Fast Breathing / Severe Chest Indrawing**: Respiratory distress / pneumonia (>60 breaths/min).
4. **Lethargy / Unconsciousness**: Baby is abnormally quiet, limp, or unresponsive.
5. **Convulsions**: Twitching, abnormal eye movements, or fits.
6. **Yellow Palms / Soles**: Severe neonatal jaundice within first 24-48 hours.

---

## Agent Guardrail Compliance
- The agent **never provides medical diagnosis** or suggests medication/treatment.
- Upon detecting any of the above danger signs, the agent:
  1. Interrupts normal record confirmation to flag the immediate danger.
  2. Speaks an urgent referral message in the worker's language: *"Yeh gambhir lakshan hai. Beneficiary ko turant nazdiki PHC le jayein."*
  3. Tags the record with `is_urgent_referral = True` and displays an emergency banner.
